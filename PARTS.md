# Recommended hardware for the real-sky phase

| Qty | Part | Recommendation |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Raspberry Pi 5 | 4 GB or 8 GB with Active Cooler |
| 1 | Power supply | Official 27 W USB-C supply |
| 1 | Camera | Raspberry Pi High Quality Camera (IMX477) |
| 1 | Lens | Manual-focus 6 mm C/CS lens to begin testing |
| 1 | Camera cable | Standard 15-pin to mini 22-pin for Pi 5 |
| 1 | microSD card | 32 GB or larger, A2-rated |
| 1 | Rigid enclosure | Internal matte-black light baffle |
| 1 | Tripod or equatorial mount | Must prevent movement during exposure |
| 1 | Calibration target | Printed checkerboard fixed to a flat board |

The HQ Camera is recommended over a phone-style autofocus module because it
accepts interchangeable manual-focus lenses and supports longer exposures. A
real star tracker also needs a rigid optical path; hand-held testing is not a
valid attitude-accuracy test.
